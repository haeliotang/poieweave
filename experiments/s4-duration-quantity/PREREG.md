# S4 Duration+Quantity Mutations — pre-registration

- Experiment ID: `s4-duration-quantity`
- Date: 2026-11-14
- Status: completed; see [REPORT.md](REPORT.md)
- Scope: one doubled process duration + one halved accepted lot.

## Question

Does Rust/independent agreement survive a timing change combined with partial
inventory loss?

## Method

7 processes × 8 accepted lots = 56 models; double the process duration, halve
the lot, compare all 9 target verdicts: 504 cases.

Prediction: agreement; mismatches investigated.

## Limitation

# S2 Resupply Frequency — pre-registration

- Experiment ID: `s2-resupply-frequency`
- Date: 2026-11-14
- Status: completed; interval 10/20/30/60 days gives 60/50/40/30 days
- Scope: resupply cadence sweep with a one-insert initial stock.

## Question

How does delivery cadence change the horizon when inserts are the limiting
consumable?

## Method

Base-08; one insert initially; pump failures every 10 days; one-insert delivery
every 10/20/30/60 days until day 90; 90-day horizon.

Prediction: shorter intervals extend survival until another limit binds.

## Limitation

One consumable; one failure cadence.

# Certificate Attestation Plumbing — pre-registration

- Experiment ID: `certificate-attestation`
- Date: 2026-11-14
- Status: completed; detached signature plumbing and tamper detection work
- Scope: ephemeral-key detached signature over the bundle digest.

## Question

Can the bundle digest be signed and verified in a way that detects tampering,
without adding a key-management/trust story yet?

## Method

At run time generate an ephemeral RSA-2048 key, sign the bundle SHA-256, verify
the clean signature, then alter the digest and require verification to fail.

Prediction: `attestation=ok`.

## Limitation

The key is ephemeral and generated by the test itself. This is plumbing and
tamper detection, not a trust anchor or third-party attestation.

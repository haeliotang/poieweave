# S2 Random Adversarial Window — pre-registration

- Experiment ID: `s2-adversarial-random-window`
- Date: 2026-11-15
- Status: completed; zero-day mass 13/200 vs 0/200 with delayed subsets
- Scope: 200 random subsets at random days 0-10, two time semantics.

## Question

Does delaying random failures reduce or remove the instantaneous zero-day mass?

## Method

Base-08 modified (no sleeves, 0.15 kg grease, 5-day repair); pump failures every
2 days; 200 random subsets at random days 0-10; 30-day horizon.

Prediction: delay reduces but may not eliminate the instantaneous zero-day mass,
while clock-advanced remains at zero.

## Limitation

Window 0-10 days; one long-repair setting.

# S2 External Resupply Deliveries — pre-registration

- Experiment ID: `s2-resupply-delivery`
- Date: 2026-11-14
- Status: completed; resupply extends survival but frequency matters
- Scope: one insert stock with/without deliveries at days 30/60.

## Question

Does discrete external resupply extend survival when the limiting resource is a
cutting-insert consumable?

## Method

Base-08; one or four inserts initially; pump failures every 10 days; optional
one-insert deliveries at days 30 and 60; 90-day horizon.

Prediction: resupply extends beyond the no-resupply one-insert horizon.

## Limitation

Two deliveries, one consumable.

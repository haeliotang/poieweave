# S2 Insert Sharpening — pre-registration

- Experiment ID: `s2-insert-regeneration`
- Date: 2026-11-14
- Status: completed; sharpening did not change the horizon
- Scope: add a modeled sharpen-insert process.

## Question

Does a modelled insert-sharpening process extend the consumable-limited
horizon?

## Method

Base-08 insert counts 2/3/4, with and without a modeled `sharpen-insert`
(worn-insert + 0.3 kWh -> cutting-insert, 1 h, gauge). 10-day failures.

Prediction: sharpening should raise the horizon for low insert counts.

## Limitation

One process model; policy is greedy.

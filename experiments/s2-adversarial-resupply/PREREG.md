# S2 Adversarial Subsets × Resupply — pre-registration

- Experiment ID: `s2-adversarial-resupply`
- Date: 2026-11-15
- Status: completed; resupply does not fix the worst subset
- Scope: 31 failure subsets at days 0/10 with/without insert deliveries.

## Question

Can external resupply raise the adversarial worst case over failure subsets?

## Method

Base-08 with one insert initially; pump failures every 10 days; insert delivered
every 10 days; all 31 non-empty subsets of five assets at day 0 or 10; compare
minimum horizons with and without resupply over 90 days.

Prediction: the worst subset may remain a capability gap rather than a material
shortage.

## Limitation

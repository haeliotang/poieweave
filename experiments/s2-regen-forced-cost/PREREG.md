# S2 Forced Regeneration Cost — pre-registration

- Experiment ID: `s2-regen-forced-cost`
- Date: 2026-11-14
- Status: completed; cost becomes binding only when recycling is forced
- Scope: no accepted sleeves/bronze, scale recycle energy.

## Question

The earlier cost experiments showed no effect because recycling was off the
critical path. If recycling is forced, does its energy cost bind?

## Method

Base-08 with all sleeve lots and bronze removed, recycle energy ×1/10/50/100,
10-day failure sequence, 90 days. Plan replay shows `recycle-bronze` is used.

Prediction: high energy multipliers eventually block on missing electricity.

## Limitation

Forced path; one cost dimension.

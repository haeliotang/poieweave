# S2 Random Adversarial × Time Semantics — pre-registration

- Experiment ID: `s2-adversarial-random-time`
- Date: 2026-11-15
- Status: completed; zero-day outcomes 129/200 vs 0/200
- Scope: 200 random subsets, 5-day repairs, on (instant vs clock-advanced).

## Question

How does clock-advanced repair change the random adversarial outcome
distribution?

## Method

Base-08 modified (no sleeves, 0.15 kg grease, 5-day repair); pump failures every
2 days; 200 random non-empty subsets at day 0; 30-day horizon; compare zero-day
count and mean.

Prediction: the instantaneous model produces many implausible zero-day collapses.

## Limitation

Subsets at day 0 only; one long-repair setting.
